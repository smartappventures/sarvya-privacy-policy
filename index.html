Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026

1. Introduction

SmartApp Ventures ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the SARVYA mobile application ("SARVYA" or "the App") handles information when you use it.

SARVYA is an offline-first utility application offering calculators, converters, and a QR code generator.

2. Information We Collect

The current version of SARVYA does not require an account or registration to use its core features.

Information you enter into calculators and converters, such as amounts, dates, measurements, and other calculation inputs, is processed locally on your device.

Content you enter to generate a QR code is used by the app to create that QR code.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

SARVYA may store app-related information locally to provide its features, including:

  • Favourite tools.
  • Calculator history.
  • App preferences, including theme settings.
  • Other settings needed for app functionality.

This information is stored by the app on your device. Android device backup settings may separately affect how device data is backed up.

You can clear app data through Android settings. Uninstalling the app may also remove locally stored data.

4. Data Sharing

SARVYA's core features do not require a user account or a SARVYA cloud database.

If you choose to share content using Android's sharing features, the information you select is handled by the destination application under its own privacy policy.

We do not sell users' personal information.

5. Advertising and Analytics

The current release of SARVYA does not include a dedicated advertising SDK or analytics SDK, based on our current app build review.

If advertising, analytics, or other third-party services are introduced in a future version, this policy will be updated to explain the relevant data practices.

6. Data Security

SARVYA is designed to process its core calculations locally on your device. We do not operate a SARVYA cloud account service for these features.

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device and any information you choose to share with other applications.

7. Children's Privacy

SARVYA is a general-purpose utility application. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children through an account registration system.

Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of mobile applications and devices.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

App preferences, favourites, and history may remain stored locally until you clear the app's data or uninstall the application.

Information shared with another application is subject to that application's retention and deletion practices.

9. Third-Party Services

The Android operating system and applications you choose to interact with may have their own privacy practices. We encourage users to review the privacy policies of those services.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when SARVYA's functionality or data practices change. Updated versions will be published on this webpage with a revised effective date.
